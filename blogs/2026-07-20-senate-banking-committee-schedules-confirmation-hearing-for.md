The Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs has scheduled a confirmation hearing for Brian Johnson, President Trump’s nominee to serve as Director of the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB). The hearing will take place on Thursday, July 23, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. EDT in Room 538 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building....
