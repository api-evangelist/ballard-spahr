Reuters reported yesterday that an internal email sent by a senior Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) official warned examination staff to avoid making “inflammatory or newsworthy” comments during examinations and cautioned that doing so could result in “the most unpleasant manner” of repercussions. Although Reuters did not publish the complete email, the excerpts it quoted have already generated considerable attention and raise important questions about the Bureau’s evolving supervisory philosophy....
