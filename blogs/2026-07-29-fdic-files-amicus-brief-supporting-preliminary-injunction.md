The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (“FDIC”) filed an amicus brief supporting the motion for a preliminary injunction filed by the National Association of Industrial Bankers (“NAIB”), the Online Lenders Alliance (“OLA”), and the American Financial Services Association (“AFSA”) in their challenge to Oregon’s recently enacted opt-out law. The FDIC explains that it has a substantial institutional interest in the case because it administers and enforces the Federal Deposit Insurance Act, including Sections 521 and 525 of the Depository Institutions Deregulation and Monetary Control Act of 19
